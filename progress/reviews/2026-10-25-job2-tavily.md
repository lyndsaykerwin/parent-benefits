# Job 2: real Tavily live offer search on the Deals tab (Oct 25, 2026)

Handoff item: `progress/handoffs/2026-10-25-v2-cloud-handoff.md`, "Final state", step 3.
(Tavily is the web-search service the app uses to look for new baby offers on 14 trusted store sites.)

## Verdict

**The real search works end to end, but what it finds is mostly not real offers.** The plumbing is proven; the quality is not demo-ready.

## What I ran

- App started on port 5052 with the real keys from `.secrets/bundle.env.local` (names checked only, no values printed), `SAMPLE_MODE=false`, `MODEL_PROVIDER=nvidia`. Stopped afterwards.
- Opened the app with `agent-browser`, picked "Try a sample family" (Baby Maya), waited for the real check to finish, opened Deals.
- Result: Deals said "Searched 14 trusted baby-offer sites · 2 new offers found today", with two "New · found today" cards under NEW TODAY.
- Screenshot: `progress/reviews/2026-10-25-job2-tavily.png`.
- I also ran the search pipeline directly in a script (same code, real Tavily and real NVIDIA AI) to time each step.

## Findings

1. **The search itself is fast and real.** Tavily answered in about 0.1 seconds with 8 results. The whole thing (search plus the AI fine-print check on up to 6 results) took about 13 seconds.

2. **First visits often show nothing new (by design, but worth knowing).** The Deals screen waits only 9 seconds for an answer, then quietly keeps the saved offers. The search keeps running in the background and the next visit gets the answer (kept for 6 hours). On the first server start, one AI call to NVIDIA took about 100 seconds, so every visit for nearly 2 minutes got "unavailable". Cause: one slow AI call holds up the whole batch (`Promise.all` in `runLiveSearch`, `bundle/server/offers/liveSearch.ts`). Not changed. Option if it matters for the demo: open Deals once a few minutes before recording, or give each AI check its own time limit.

3. **Junk, plus a privacy problem: FIXED.** The first real run returned 6 cards; 4 were strangers' personal baby registries on `my.babylist.com` (for example "Ashley's Baby Registry"), showing real people's names. They counted as "Babylist" because any address under babylist.com was allowed.
   - Fix (small, in the search code only): `PERSONAL_PAGE_HOSTS = ['my.babylist.com']` in `bundle/server/offers/liveSearch.ts`. Tavily is told to skip those pages (`excludeDomains`), and `storeForUrl` drops any that slip through.
   - New test: `bundle/server/__tests__/liveSearchPersonalPages.test.ts`. It passes, along with `offerRoutes.test.ts` and `liveFinds.test.ts` (30 tests).
   - Note: another helper has unsaved changes in the same file (saving live finds to `offer_checks`). My edit only touched `storeForUrl` and one search option. Before the fix, those registry pages would also have been saved to the shared list.

4. **Still not real offers (NOT fixed; it's a design issue).** After the fix, the two cards were ordinary product listings: "Fisher-Price ... Baby Body Wash" (Walmart) and an "aden + anais ... Muslin Baby Blanket" (Amazon, a Chinese-language page address). All links open (HTTP 200), but neither one is a deal. Three reasons, all in `liveSearch.ts`:
   - The search phrase is very broad, and `timeRange: 'month'` keeps only pages published recently. The store help pages on the trusted list (welcome-box rules and so on) are rarely dated as new, so product and blog pages win.
   - Tavily brings back only about 150 to 260 characters of text per page, so the "fine print" the AI judges is almost empty.
   - The Fine Print Court only asks "is there a catch?", never "is this actually an offer?". Pages with no catch get approved, with the text "Our fine-print check found nothing that makes it costly."
   - Suggested next step (not done, bigger than a small fix): add an "is this an offer for new parents?" check before the court, drop `timeRange` or move to a narrower query per store, and require `rawContent`. The other choice is to keep live search off for the demo and show saved offers only.

5. **Small UI note.** In the browser, `agent-browser`'s normal click on "View my results" did nothing three times in a row (a script click worked). Possibly something covers the button, or it was the live reloads from other helpers' edits. Worth one manual check.

## Round 2: live search fixed (Lyndsay approved: "add an 'is this an offer?' check, narrow the search, and stop one slow AI call from blocking the rest")

### Result (real mode, port 5052, sample family Maya, agent-browser)

Deals said "Searched 14 trusted baby-offer sites · 4 new offers found today". **All 4 are real offers a new parent can claim:**

| Card | Page | Court said |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon: Baby Registry Welcome Box | amazon.com welcome-box rules (help page) | Approved with a catch: while supplies last |
| Enfamil: Free Baby Formula Samples & Coupons | enfamil.com/baby-formula-coupons-samples | Approved with a catch: needs a phone call / app |
| Walmart: Free Baby Boxes & Baby Registry | walmart.com/cp/baby-registry | Approved |
| Enfamil: Claim Your Enfamil Wonder Box Gift | enfamil.com/wonderbox-gift | Approved with a catch: needs an app |

The Enfamil and Walmart links open normally (HTTP 200). The Amazon link returns 403 to a script; Amazon blocks scripts, and it is the same page as one already on the trusted list. No product listings, articles or registries. The first Deals visit got a live answer, and no "unavailable" answers were logged. None of the cards names a dollar amount ("True value not stated").
Screenshot: `progress/reviews/2026-10-25-job2-tavily-after.png`.

### What changed (all in the search code; tests written first)

1. **"Is this an offer?" check**, before the court reads the fine print:
   - A quick code screen drops product listings (`/dp/`, `/ip/`, `/p/`), people's own registries, blog posts, forum threads, how-to and list articles (for example "How to get free baby stuff", "8 freebies…"), Babylist's article pages, and any page that never mentions anything free, off, a reward, a gift or a coupon.
   - Then the AI is asked one short question: answer OFFER or NOT_OFFER. Anything but a clear OFFER is dropped, including no answer at all. A missed offer costs little; a junk card costs trust.
   - The AI now gets the full page text (`rawContent`, up to 6,000 characters) when Tavily has it, instead of the 150–260 character snippet.
2. **Narrower search.** Two offer-focused search phrases now run side by side: welcome boxes, then rewards, samples and coupons. Each costs one Tavily credit, so two per run, and the answer is kept for 6 hours. The "past month only" limit is gone; it was the main reason only product pages and articles came back. Results take turns between the two phrases, so one phrase cannot fill all 6 checked slots. The same page under different spellings (http/www/tracking codes) counts once. The trusted-sites limit and the personal-page block are kept.
3. **No more waiting on one slow AI call.** Each result's checks get 20 seconds; a slower one is dropped and the rest still show. A full run now takes about 11–13 seconds.
4. **The 9-second Deals limit no longer matters in practice:**
   - The server starts the search as soon as it starts (real mode with a key only, never in practice mode or tests), so the answer is ready before anyone opens Deals.
   - After 6 hours, the old answer comes back at once while a fresh search runs behind it, with no wait. A new day always searches again.
   - No screen changes.
5. The `storeForUrl` comment is back above its own function.
