## Failures and root causes

**A. Back from Account leaves the app (step 2).**
- Account is not a separate page. It is a hidden 6th tab state: `openAccount = () => change('account')` in `bundle/client/src/pages/tabs/VersionB.tsx:106`. `change()` (line 62) only calls `setTab` and `saveTab`, so nothing new is added to the browser's history and the web address does not change.
- `bundle/client/src/pages/tabs/AccountTab.tsx` has no "‹ Back" button. The gear locker does have one.
- What happens: from Account, the phone's Back button skips past Account and every earlier tab and leaves the app altogether (in testing it went to a blank page). The only way out is to tap a bottom tab, and none of them is lit up while Account is open.
- Fix: add a Back button that returns to the tab you came from (and its scroll spot), and/or add a history entry when Account opens.

**B. The receipt photo sometimes fails with a misleading message (step 5).**
- I ran the same steps the server runs, outside the app (`readPhoto`), and saw the real error: NVIDIA replied `503 ResourceExhausted: Worker local total request limit reached (16/16)`. That means NVIDIA's shared free service was full, so it refused the request.
- A receipt needs two calls to the model. The first worked and the second was refused. On the next try both worked. So the problem is NVIDIA running out of capacity now and then, not our settings.
- The app shows that refusal as "needs the live model, which is not switched on here". `bundle/server/ingest/photo.ts:459-461` turns every kind of model failure into `NEEDS_LIVE_MODEL_MESSAGE` (line 19). That wording is wrong in real mode. The retry in `server/adapters/llm.ts` (`call`, about line 200) waits only 1–3 seconds and tries once.
- Fix: when the failure reason is `server` or `timeout`, say "Busy right now, try again in a minute". Consider a longer pause before the retry. Before recording the demo, warm up the receipt scan or have a retry ready.

- "What Bundle has learned from parents" shows raw internal codes: "graded reject", "approve_with_catch", "required_membership".
- Home still shows Maya's built-in money-back items (Frida thermometer $28.99 + Pack 'n Play price drop $20). The scanned receipt's thermometer is marked FSA-eligible, with no dollar amount on the scan screen.
- When the page is scrolled down, the family menu closes as soon as the page scrolls. That is fine for a person, because the header is not pinned and they scroll up to reach it anyway.

They worked first time with a normal mouse click, so this looks like a quirk of the testing tool, not a bug people will hit. The date box also had to be typed one digit at a time, which is also a testing-tool quirk.
